Related project · active research

SO-101: from simulation to a physical arm.

A vision-language-action policy, demonstrations, reinforcement learning, and a plan for controlled real-robot evaluation.

This project builds on the foundations in simulation, control and evaluation. It uses a different robot, tasks, observations and success criteria from the Panda PickCube studies. Results should be read within their own experimental protocols.

Status: Simulation, demonstration, fine-tuning, reinforcement-learning and robot-side infrastructure have been developed. Reliable VLA improvement and real-robot task performance remain to be established. The working repository is private.

The system

One research pipeline, four stages.

01

Simulate & demonstrate

An SO-101 simulation with PickPlace, Push and Stack tasks, a scripted oracle and recorded demonstrations.

02

Fine-tune a VLA

Adapt a pretrained vision-language-action model using a small demonstration set, then evaluate on seen positions, shifts and new objects.

03

Test reinforcement learning

Use simulation feedback to study whether policy post-training improves the fine-tuned starting point.

04

Evaluate on hardware

Calibrate the arm and cameras, validate safety and teleoperation, then compare policies in real-world trials.

Research status

Building toward measured robot learning.

Simulation and model fine-tuning are active areas of work. Reinforcement-learning comparisons and real-robot evaluation remain in progress. Results will be published when ready, together with their evaluation methods and limitations.

What exists

Simulation tasks and demonstrations, VLA fine-tuning/evaluation work, a tested RL-loop scaffold, and robot-side safety and calibration code.

What remains open

Verified SFT-versus-RL comparisons, independent training-seed replication, completed hardware validation and real-world task trials.

Connected, with separate evidence

One portfolio, distinct experiments.

Foundations studies explore control and Panda manipulation; SO-101 explores VLA post-training and eventual physical deployment. Each project retains its own task definitions and results.
